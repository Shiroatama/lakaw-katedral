## Title

A PILGRIM’S GUIDE THROUGH THE NAGA METROPOLITAN CATHEDRAL

## Welcome

Welcome, dear pilgrim and visitor!

You are entering more than a historic church. You are entering a living story of faith.

For more than four centuries, the Naga Metropolitan Cathedral has stood at the heart of Bicol’s Catholic life. The present Cathedral, begun in 1808 and completed in 1843, is a magnificent heritage church distinguished by its massive masonry, Romanesque character, graceful arches, twin towers, and majestic dome.

As you journey through the Cathedral, we humbly invite you to: Walk slowly. Look closely. Pray deeply because every image, arch, bell, stone, and sacred space has a story to tell you.

## Things to do in Naga Metropolitan Cathedral

### 1 | Enter with the Saints

**Begin your journey with prayer.**

As you pass through the main door, invoke St. John the Evangelist, Patron Saint of the Cathedral Parish, and St. Peter Baptist, Patron Saint of the Archdiocese of Caceres.

Ask them to walk with you, protect you, and lead you closer to Christ.

### 2 | See How the Gospel Came to Bicol

**Let the mural tell you the story.**

Pause before the mural of the distinguished Bicolano artist Raul Alcomendas.

Discover through his work the story of Bicol’s evangelization—the missionaries who came, the communities they served, and the generations who received and passed on the faith.

You are part of a story that began centuries ago.

### 3 | Discover the Cathedra

**Where the Archbishop teaches and shepherds.**

Find the Cathedra—the Archbishop’s Chair.

It is more than a chair of honor. It symbolizes the Archbishop’s teaching and pastoral authority and reminds us that the Cathedral is the mother church of the Archdiocese.

### 4 | Remember the First Archbishop

**Honor a shepherd who came before us.**

Pray before the tomb of +Most Rev. Pedro S. Santos, the first Archbishop of Caceres.

Give thanks for his life and ministry, and remember in prayer all the bishops, priests, religious, and lay faithful who have helped build and sustain the Church of Bicol.

### 5 | Meet Ina

**Entrust your heart and your loved ones to her.**

Come before the Novenary Image of Our Lady of Peñafrancia.

Stand in prayer. Reverently kiss her sacred image. Entrust to Ina your family, loved ones, dreams, burdens, and intentions.

“Ina, lead us always to Jesus.”

### 6 | Look Up and Remember the Missionaries

**Lift your eyes toward the dome.**

Above the sanctuary, discover the religious orders that evangelized Bicol and the founders who inspired their missionary work.

As you look upward, offer a prayer that the Church may always have generous hearts willing to bring the Gospel to others.

### 7 | Be Still Before Christ

**The greatest treasure is not what you see—it is Whom you encounter.**

Behold the silver-clad Tabernacle below the main retablo.

Inside the Tabernacle lies the heart of the Cathedral: Jesus Christ, truly present in the Blessed Sacrament.

Stop. Be silent. Stay with Him.

### 8 | Climb the Belfries

**See Naga from a different perspective.**

Ask the Cathedral staff to guide you to the belfries. Ascend carefully and safely.

From above, admire the Holy Rosary Minor Seminary on your right. On your left, take in the busy Elias Angeles Street, the Archbishop’s Residence, and the historic façade of the renowned Universidad de Santa Isabel.

Look over the city and remember how generations have grown around this ancient center of faith.

### 9 | Ring the Campana de la Paz

**Let the voice of history speak again.**

Visit the Heritage Bells and Candle Garden.

Light a candle. Offer a prayer. Then, where permitted, ring the Campana de la Paz, the Cathedral’s oldest preserved heritage bell dating from 1743.

As its sound rises, remember the countless Bicolanos who heard church bells calling them to prayer, worship, celebration, and service.

From the Garden, Four bells. Centuries of faith. One continuing story.

### 10 | Enter Gethsemane

**Stay with Jesus in His hour of prayer.**

Visit the Adoration Chapel inspired by the atmosphere of the Garden of Gethsemane.

Remain with Jesus in silence.

Place before Him whatever you carry in your heart like your fears, hopes, gratitude, struggles, and dreams.

“Lord, stay with me.”

### 11 | Touch the Stones of History

**Let the old bricks speak.**

At the Cathedral Parish Office and Rectory, see the historic brick arches that support the structure.

Where appropriate, gently touch these enduring witnesses of time and remember the priests, families, pilgrims, and parishioners who have passed through these same spaces.

### 12 | Enter the Holy Family Chapel

**Where life begins, love is sealed, and mercy is received**

Visit the Holy Family Chapel, an intimate place of faith, family, and prayer. Here, children are welcomed through Baptism, the vows of husband and wife are sealed in Holy Matrimony, and during the Peñafrancia Novenary in September, penitents come to receive God’s forgiveness in the Sacrament of Reconciliation.

Be awed by its beautiful stained-glass windows depicting the Flight of the Holy Family into Egypt and the Holy Family of Nazareth, with St. Joseph in his humble vocation as a carpenter.

God chose a family and sanctified love, work, and ordinary family life.

Pause, pray, and entrust your own family to Jesus, Mary, and Joseph.

### 13 | Meet Ina at the Porta Mariae

**A landmark of Naga. A monument of love for Ina.**

Take your photograph at the Porta Mariae, one of Naga City’s beloved landmarks.

Its three floors symbolize the centuries of Ina’s heavenly protection and the enduring love and devotion of the Bicolanos.

Visit the Porta Mariae Museum and discover rare artifacts connected with the devotion to Our Lady of Peñafrancia.

Then ascend to the third floor.

There, look up and encounter the towering image of Ina, seemingly watching over the City of Naga and all her children and devotees.

Pause beneath her gaze. Entrust yourself to her care.

### 14 | Rise to the Quadricentennial Dome

**See the Cathedral as a whole.**

Visit the Quadricentennial Dome and marvel at the beauty of the Cathedral compound.

From this vantage point, see how the sacred spaces, historic structures, and living community come together as one heritage of faith.

### 15 | Celebrate Christ in the Eucharist

**The journey finds its summit at the altar.**

Go to the Parish Office to offer for a Holy Mass to be offered for your thanksgiving, special intentions, for your family, or departed loved ones.

There is no better way to complete a pilgrimage than to offer for a Mass and celebrate Christ in the Eucharist.

### 16 | Take a Piece of Your Pilgrimage Home

**Let the memory become a reminder of faith.**

Visit the Cathedral Extension Office and Souvenir Shop for affordable religious items and devotional materials.

Choose something that will remind you not simply of the place you visited, but of the prayer you offered and the Christ you encountered.
